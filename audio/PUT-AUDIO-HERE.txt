Put your own audio here (.mp3, .wav, .ogg or .m4a), then name it in js/data.js:
  settings.music.file  -> your own soundtrack instead of the built-in song
  sounds               -> your own sound effects (play, clip, close, rewind, insert)
  a clip's media     -> { type: "audio", src: "audio/file.mp3", caption: "..." }
See README.md.
